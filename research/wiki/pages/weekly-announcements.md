# Weekly Announcements (weekly-announcement posts)

These are the **weekly parent-announcement posts** (家庭聯絡事項 Weekly Announcement),
published as EN and ZH pairs. They are the school's main channel for week-by-week
notices. The index page is `/upcoming-event/` (menu: "Weekly Announcement").

## Index page: `/upcoming-event/`

- **Title**: Upcoming Event
- **Headings on page**: 家庭聯絡事項 Weekly Announcement · 本週 / 上週 (This Week / Last Week)
- The index dynamically shows the current week's and last week's announcement links.

## Pattern of weekly posts

- Slug pattern: `wNN_news_eng-*` / `wNN_news_chn-*` (NN = week number).
- Each week has an **English** and a **Chinese** post; the PDF for each is attached
  as a downloadable file (e.g. `W25_news_Eng.pdf`, `W25_news_Chn.pdf`).
- The posts themselves are **near-empty** in the crawl (body is just the nav) — the
  real content lives in the attached PDFs.
- Week numbers observed: **W01–W04** (2025-26 year, uploaded 2026-09/10), **W12–W29**
  (2024-25 year, uploaded 2025-12 … 2026-05), and the legacy **2018-19 week 07** pair.
- Suffix numbers (`-2`,`-3`,`-4`,`-5`,`-6`) are WordPress post-URL dedup suffixes
  (duplicate slugs) — not different content.

## Notes

- The **actual announcement text** is inside the PDFs (the post HTML bodies are empty
  in the crawl). Treat the PDFs as the source of truth for weekly notices.
- **Duplicate files**: `W18_news_Chn-1.pdf` == `W18_news_Chn.pdf` (same bytes),
  `W18_news_Eng-1.pdf` == `W18_news_Eng.pdf`, `W29_news_Chn-1/2.pdf` == `W29_news_Chn.pdf`,
  `W29_news_Eng-1.pdf` == `W29_news_Eng.pdf` — see `assets.md` duplicate flags.
- **Spam**: the two 2018-19 W07 posts contain thousands of drug/pharmacy/casino
  comment-spam entries (see `gaps.md`); ignore their comment sections entirely.
