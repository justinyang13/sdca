> SDCA is a non-profit educational organization, Scrip sale profits are used to fund
> school activities. Every student is charged a refundable Scrip Deposit **$50** when
> he/she is enrolled. Scrip are gift cards from grocery stores, department stores, retail
> stores or restaurants. You can use these gift cards at stores, and our school gets a
> certain percentage back as rebate from the retailers. Enrolled families are encouraged to
> purchase scrip, and when the accumulated amount reach a set level for the school year
> (**$700** or more for the first enrolled child of the family; **$600** or more for the
> second child; and **$500** or more for the third and each of the rest enrolled child in
> the family), refund in Scrip of your choice(s) may be picked up on any Scrip Day upon
> fulfillment. You have the entire school year to fulfill and exceed the refund requirement,
> while helping to raise fund for the school at the same time.

> Scrip sale, Scrip deposit refund and service fee refund are available at the office on
> any Scrip Day (first available Sunday of each month and pre-registration days).
