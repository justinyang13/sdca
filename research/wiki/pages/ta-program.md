## Purpose

Recruitment page for high-school **Teaching Assistant** volunteers who help teachers
during school hours and earn community-service hours.

## Full text content (verbatim)

> San Diego Chinese Academy welcomes local high school students with interest and
> dedication of assisting our teachers with both clerical and instructional work during
> school hours. Each teaching assistant can be tasked with various responsibilities which
> include, but not limits to classroom activities, school events assistants, equipment set
> up and/or preparation of materials, and lead students as needed by the assigned teachers.
> Bilingual in Chinese is preferred and you will also improve your Chinese along the way.

> The school track their volunteer hours and each TA will receive a certificate at the end
> of the school year. This TA program has long been successfully providing support to our
> teacher while our teaching assistants gain leadership opportunities and earn their
> community volunteer hours which works toward their college applications.

> Join our TA team! Fill out the application below. Or Contact Us should you have any
> questions.
