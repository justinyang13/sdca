跳舞課 Dance with U-Jam

大家一起來跳舞！ 星期天下午三點到四點請來 MS112 參加舞蹈課。

Come dance with us, World of Dance U-Jam is here! Come to this addictive dance cardio fitness workout that fuses the hottest world beats with fun choreography that will make you move, sweat, and smile! Remember to bring water bottle, towel, comfortable outfit, exercise shoes, bandanna (optional) and get ready to sweat!
