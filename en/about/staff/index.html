校長的話 Words from the Principal

2018 年是中華學苑建校史上至關重要的一年，這一年中華學苑年滿三十歲了。2月11日，爲慶祝建校三十週年，我們舉行了隆重的慶典活動。活動當天天氣晴朗，艷陽高照，精彩纷呈的节目表演，以及規模盛大的園遊會吸引了人山人海。園遊會設有令人垂涎欲滴的風味美食攤位，以及趣味十足的遊戲攤位，現場人頭攒動，熱鬧非凡。到場的來賓以及學生家長無不誇讚，真是好吃又好玩。為本次活動學校還精心編輯、出版了中華學苑三十週年校慶特刊 。更多..

2018 is a very important year for San Diego Chinese Academy because the Academy has turned 30 years old. On February 11, 2018, we immensely celebrated the 30th anniversary of the Chinese Academy. There were wonderful performances on that day. The scale of the school fair was lively and impressive. There were lots of guests, food stalls, and game booths at the event. The food was delicious and everyone had a great time. We also edited and published a special edition yearbook for the 30th anniversary. Read More..

Each year we send our teachers to various training to make sure their knowledge and teaching techniques are up to date. Our teachers work closely with parents and pay attention to each learner’s progress.

What makes SDCA unique?

Versatile teaching methods: a variety of activities are used to stimulate learning and practical use of the Chinese language.

Outstanding faculty: experienced, well trained and dedicated teachers make all the differences.

Small class size: student number for most of our classes is under 20. We provide TAs to some classes to make sure each student gets quality attention from the teacher.

Bilingual system: enabling students with no prior exposure to learn Chinese.

Non-profit language school teaching Mandarin and Chinese culture since 1988.
